Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Scorepion. We're committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our football prediction app.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your username, email address, and favorite leagues when you create your account. We also automatically collect data about your predictions, match selections, in-app activity, and basic device information (device type, OS version) to maintain and improve the app's performance and personalization.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve Scorepion, including running competitions, calculating your leaderboard standings, personalizing your experience, sending match reminders and game updates, analyzing user trends, and detecting fraudulent activity. Your data helps us understand how you interact with the app so we can make it better.

Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share aggregated, anonymized statistics with analytics providers to understand app usage patterns. We comply with legal requests from authorities and may disclose information when required by law or to protect the safety and rights of Scorepion and its users.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Some data may be retained for compliance, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes as required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, as well as withdraw consent to data processing. You also have the right to data portability and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@scorepion.fans.

Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data in transit and at rest. While we take security seriously, no system is completely secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to our security team rather than disclosing it publicly.

Children's Privacy

Scorepion is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete the information and terminate the child's account immediately.

International Users

Your data may be processed and stored in different countries, including countries outside your country of residence. By using Scorepion, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of origin, which may have different data protection rules.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we'll notify you by email or prominent notice within the app. Your continued use of Scorepion after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at support@scorepion.fans. We'll respond to your inquiry within 30 days.
