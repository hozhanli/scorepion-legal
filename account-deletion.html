Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Scorepion account and data

Delete your account in the app

You can permanently delete your Scorepion account and all associated data at any time, directly from the app:

  1. Open Scorepion and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and associated data are permanently removed.

Prefer email?

If you can't access the app, email us at support@scorepion.fans from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." We'll verify your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your account credentials (email address, username)
  • Your profile and preferences (favorite leagues, settings)
  • Your predictions, match selections, and in-app activity
  • Your group memberships and leaderboard standings
  • Your stats, streaks, and points history

What we may retain

After deletion, we do not keep your personal data except where retention is required by law, or is necessary for fraud prevention, security, or to resolve disputes. Any such data is kept only for as long as legally required and is then deleted. Aggregated or anonymized data that can no longer identify you may be retained.

Contact

Questions about account or data deletion? Contact support@scorepion.fans. See also our Privacy Policy.
